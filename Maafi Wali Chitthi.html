Maafi Wali Chitthi

Naraaz ho mujhse?

Ek chhoti si chitthi hai, sirf tumhare liye. Pehle photo dekho, phir meri baat sun lo.

Kuch lines meri taraf se

Tumhari naraazgi bhi mujhe pyaari lagti hai,

Bas ye chuppi mujhe sabse bhaari lagti hai.

Gussa kar lo, daant bhi lo, sab kuch sah lunga,

Bas baat karna band na karna, warna kaise rah lunga.

Tumhe manaana mujhe sach me aata nahi,

Par tumse door rehna bhi mujhe bhaata nahi.

Chai wahi hai, raaste wahi, mausam bhi wahi hai,

Bas ek tumhari hansi hai jo aaj kahin nahi hai.

Ab bas kar do ye ruthna, mujhe tumse kuch kehna hai,

Ladna bhi tumhi se hai, aur tumhi ke saath rehna hai.

Maaf kar diya?

Jo bhi jawab ho, bas mujhse baat kar lena.

— tumhara dost
